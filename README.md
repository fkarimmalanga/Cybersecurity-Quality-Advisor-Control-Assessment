# 🛡️ Cybersecurity Quality Advisor – Control Assessment & Risk Reporting

## 🎯 Objective
Simulate the role of a Cybersecurity Quality Advisor by assessing control implementation, managing risk, and providing recommendations aligned with **NIST 800-37**, **NIST 800-53**, and **NIST CSF** frameworks.

## 📘 Project Overview
This project demonstrates a day-in-the-life workflow for validating controls, managing risk, and ensuring continuous monitoring of IT systems and applications. The simulation focuses on a financial application scenario.

### 🔑 Key Goals
- ✅ Verify asset inventory and classification  
- ✅ Assess control implementation and gather evidence  
- ✅ Identify control gaps and assign risk levels  
- ✅ Recommend remediation and track timelines  
- ✅ Provide a summarized dashboard/report for management

## 🗓️ Day-by-Day Workflow

### Day 1 – Categorize & Select (Data Collection)
- Sent access requests to review asset inventory for the Finance application.  
- Coordinated with the CMDB and IT Asset teams to verify system ownership, classification, and documentation.  
- Worked with the system/app owner to understand application purpose, data types, and user roles.  
- Selected initial controls based on criticality and impact (e.g., AC, CM, AU families).

### Day 2 – Implement & Assess (Evidence Collection)
- Collected evidence (simulated) such as screenshots, patch reports, vulnerability scan reports, access review logs, EDR logs.  
- Assessed whether controls were implemented correctly.  
- Identified gaps such as missing access reviews, incomplete vulnerability reports, or partial control implementation.

### Day 3 – Recommend & Align (Assess / Authorize)
- Documented control gaps and provided recommendations based on severity (High, Medium, Low).  
- Examples of recommendations:  
  - 🔒 Enforce periodic access reviews for admin accounts  
  - 📝 Enable full audit log retention  
  - ✅ Ensure vulnerability scan reports show remediation completed  
- Reviewed recommendations with system/app owners and IT Asset team to confirm feasibility.  
- Provided finalized recommendations to the CMDB team for updates.

### Day 4 – Follow-Up & Continuous Monitoring
- Coordinated with system/app owner, IT Asset team, and CMDB team to verify that recommended controls were implemented.  
- Checked that evidence was accurate and CMDB was updated accordingly.  
- Monitored high-risk items to ensure timely remediation.

### Day 5 – Reporting & Closure
- Summarized all findings into a report showing control gaps, evidence status, risk levels, and remediation timelines.  
- Documented lessons learned to improve future assessments.  
- Prepared a risk summary dashboard for management visibility.

## 📂 Deliverables
- Sheet 1: Control Mapping & Recommendations  
- Sheet 2: Control Assessment & Evidence Matrix  
- Sheet 3: Risk Summary & Reporting

## 💡 Key Skills Demonstrated
- Control assessment and validation  
- Risk prioritization and management (High, Medium, Low)  
- Knowledge of RMF (NIST 800-37), NIST 800-53 controls, and CSF functions  
- Collaboration with system/app owners, IT Asset team, and CMDB team  
- Documentation for audit and compliance readiness

## 🧠 Lessons Learned
- Validating evidence is key to confirming that controls were implemented.  
- Collaboration with System/App Owners, IT Asset, and CMDB teams ensures data accuracy.  
- Prioritizing risks (High/Medium/Low) improves focus and remediation efficiency.  
- Maintaining updated documentation supports continuous monitoring and compliance.

## ⚙️ Framework & Tools
- **Framework:** NIST 800-37 (Risk Management Framework)  
- **Tools:** Microsoft Excel, CMDB, Risk Register  
- **Focus Areas:** Risk Analysis, Continuous Monitoring, Control Validation
